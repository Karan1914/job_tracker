## Purpose

This tool was built strictly for **educational and personal research purposes** — specifically to help the author track publicly available job postings across SF Bay Area companies during an active job search.

## Limitations of Use

- This tool only accesses **publicly available** job board APIs (Greenhouse, Lever) and career pages.  
- It does **not** bypass authentication, scrape private data, or circumvent any robots.txt or terms of service in a harmful way.
- It is **not** intended for commercial use, bulk data collection for resale, or any purpose other than personal career research.
- The company list, match scoring weights, and TC estimates are based on the author's personal research and public sources. They are **approximate** and may be outdated.

## If You Adapt This Project

If you fork or adapt this project for your own job search:
1. Update `config.py` with your own target roles, skills, and email.
2. Update `matcher.py` to reflect your own resume and experience.
3. Update `companies.py` to target companies relevant to your own search.
4. Respect each company's terms of service when accessing their career pages.
